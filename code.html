About The Artist

“I photograph people, stories & moments.”

Over twelve years of documentary and editorial practice, I have rejected contrived posturing in favor of raw cadence. Photography, to me, is not the creation of an illusion, but the patient acknowledgment of an existing truth.

Specializing in heirloom wedding monographs, fine art portraiture, and architectural narratives, each commission is treated as a limited printed work bound to outlast digital impermanence.

Methodology

My Approach

01 / DISCIPLINE
OBSERVE

Finding the moments that happen naturally. Without intervention, waiting until the subject forgets the lens entirely.

02 / ARCHITECTURE
FRAME

Creating compositions with intention. Harmonizing negative space, tactile materials, and ambient daylight into balanced equilibrium.

03 / ARTIFACT
PRESERVE

Turning temporary moments into lasting physical images. Fine archival papers, non-acidic cotton rags, and hand-bound cloth albums.

The Protocol

Client Experience

01

DISCOVER

Initial consultation to discuss the context, emotional goals, locations, and aesthetic expectations of the commission.

02

PLAN

Lighting reconnaissance, site surveys, wardrobe palette alignment, and shot-list curation without constraining spontaneity.

03

SHOOT

An immersive, fluid production where we honor the organic momentum of the day using natural light and deliberate framing.

04

DELIVER

Archival master scans, bespoke web vault access, and tailored physical linen-bound photobooks delivered to your home.
